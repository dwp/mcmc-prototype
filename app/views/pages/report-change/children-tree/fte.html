When do Child Maintenance payments end?

Regular ongoing child maintenance payments end when the child leaves approved education or training, and the eligibility for Child Benefit stops.

For children leaving education in summer, Child Benefit usually stops at the end of August.

For more information, visit when does Child Maintenance stop (opens in a new tab).

When your child leaves education

You do not need to contact the Child Maintenance Service.

HMRC will tell us when the eligibility for a child benefit ends, this is normally when a child leaves approved education or training.

We will then contact you and either:

  • recalculate your child maintenance if there are other qualifying children on your case, or there is unpaid child maintenance (arrears)
  • close your case if your child is the last and only qualifying child on your case

You can report changes to your child's circumstances by visiting report changes to child benefit (opens in a new tab).

If you do not know the situation or Child Benefit is not being paid

You can tell us when a child leaves approved education or training (opens in a new tab).
