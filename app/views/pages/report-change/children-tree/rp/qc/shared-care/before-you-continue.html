Before you continue

You will need:

  • the date {{ data['childrentree-whichChild'] }}'s nights with the other parent changed
  • how many nights {{ data['childrentree-whichChild'] }} stays with the other parent now
  • if {{ data['childrentree-whichChild'] }} stays overnight with a foster carer or in a care home
  • details of any documents you have as evidence

Asking the other parent

We will ask the other parent about {{ data['childrentree-whichChild'] }}'s nights with you and make our decision using the information we have from both of you.

Sending evidence

You should send evidence if you can. This will support your change in case you and the other parent disagree. We will ask you to upload it to your account within 7 days of reporting the change.

If you do not have evidence, you can still report the change and we will make a decision using the information we have.

If you and the other parent agree on the change, we do not need to look at your evidence.

What we can accept

We can only accept:

  • evidence of Child Benefit payments, for example bank statements
  • evidence the child is in full time education, for example a legal document or letter

We cannot accept:

  • communication that is only between you and the other parent
  • photos of your child
  • screenshots of social media
  • calendar entries

Your payments may change

Reporting a new child may affect your child maintenance payments.
