Before you continue

You are telling us the other parent of Bob and 2 others has stopped financially supporting a child.

This could be because the other parent or their partner has either stopped:

  • being the main carer for a child
  • making payments privately to another main carer outside the Child Maintenance Service

Asking the other parent

We will ask the other parent about Bill's day-to-day care and make our decision using the information we have from both of you.

Sending evidence

You should send evidence if you can. This will support your change in case you and the other parent disagree. We will ask you to upload it to your account within 7 days of reporting the change.

If you do not have evidence, you can still report the change and we will make a decision using the information we have.

If you and the other parent agree on the change, we do not need to look at your evidence.

What we can accept

Examples of what we can accept:

  • legal documents - for example, a court order
  • communication showing your day-to-day responsibility - for example, letter from your child's GP
  • records of money you have spent on your child's care - for example, receipts

We cannot accept:

  • communication that is only between you and the other parent
  • photos of your child
  • screenshots of social media
  • calendar entries

Your payments may change

RThe change you report may affect your child maintenance payments.
