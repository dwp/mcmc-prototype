Before you continue

You are telling us you:

  • have a new child you need to make payments for
  • you already have {{ data['whoReceives'] }} with your new child's other parent

You will need:

  • your child's full name
  • the date your child was born

Asking the other parent

We will ask the other parent about your new child and make our decision using the information we have from both of you.

Sending evidence

You should send evidence to show you are eligible to pay child maintenance. Evidence will also support your answers in case the other parent disagrees.

We will ask you to upload the evidence to your account within 14 days of reporting the new child.

If you do not have evidence, you can still report the child and we will make a decision using the information we have.

What we can accept

We can only accept:

  • evidence of Child Benefit payments, for example bank statements
  • evidence the child is in full time education, for example a legal document or letter

We cannot accept:

  • communication that is only between you and the other parent
  • photos of your child
  • screenshots of social media
  • calendar entries

Your payments may change

Reporting a new child may affect your child maintenance payments.
