Supporting another child

To make sure your financial responsibility to all your children is accurately reflected in your payment calculations. you may need to provide evidence you are supporting another child. Evidence can include a Child Benefit award notice to prove you or your current partner qualify for Child Benefit.

If you are financially supporting a child from a different relationship it may affect the amount of child maintenance you pay.
