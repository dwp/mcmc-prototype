Before you continue

You will need the new child's:

  • full name
  • date of birth
  • National Insurance or Child Benefit number, if they have one

If you have a private arrangement, you will also need how much you pay and how often.

Sending evidence

You should send evidence if you can. We will ask you to upload it to your account within X days.

If you do not have evidence, you can still report and we will make a decision using the information we have.

What we can accept

Evidence can include:

  • proof of entitlement to Child Benefit
  • a Child Benefit award notice
  • bank statements showing your Child Benefit payments
  • payslips, where you do not claim Child Benefit
  • a UK court order
  • >a written maintenance agreement drawn up by a solicitor
  • an informal written agreement signed by you and the parent receiving your private payments
  • a foreign court order
  • a liability order set under a foreign state's child support scheme

Your payments may change

The child you report may affect your child maintenance payments.
