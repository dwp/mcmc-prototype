Asking the other parent

We will ask the other parent about {{ data['whichChild'] }}'s nights with you and make our decision using the information we have from both of you.

Sending evidence

You should send evidence if you can. This will support your answers in case you and the other parent disagree. We will ask you to upload it to your account within X days.

If you do not have evidence, you can still report the change and we will make a decision using the information we have.

If you and the other parent agree, we do not need to look at your evidence.

What we can accept

We can only accept:

  • a current court order
  • a formal agreement - for example, drawn up by a solicitor
  • another official document - for example, reports from Children and Family Court Advisory and Support Service (CAFCASS) or social services

We cannot accept:

  • communication that is only between you and the other parent
  • photos of your child
  • screenshots of social media
  • calendar entries

Your payments may change

Reporting a new child may affect your child maintenance payments.
